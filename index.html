Bomb Risk

Cross Locked Lite

  • Score points by completing as many lines and Xs as possible with tiles of just your color, Red.
  • Prevent the opponent, Blue, from doing the same.
  • On your turn, place as many tiles as you can in sequences like 4-5-6 or like kind like 4-4-4.
  • For each tile played, you receive a fresh tile. Your turn ends when you get hit with a bomb or choose End Turn.
  • Bombs remove 1 or 2 tiles from the board.
  • Score 25 points for each short line, 50 for a long line, 100 for a small X, and 200 for a large X. No line points are awarded on a completed X.
  • Each game has 2 rounds. Each round ends when the board is filled. High score wins.
