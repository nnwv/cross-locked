Cross Locked Lite Rules

  • Red only plays red 1-5 tiles. Blue CPU only plays blue 1-5 tiles.
  • Each round starts with random center tiles. Round 1 starts with Blue CPU; round 2 starts with Red.
  • Tiles must fit a center line as matching numbers or a sequence.
  • After every placed tile, press Draw to reveal a number tile, Red Bomb, or Blue Bomb.
  • Short lines score 25. Long lines score 50. A full small cross scores 100 and a full big cross scores 200, with no extra line points for that cross.
