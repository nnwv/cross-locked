Cross Locked Lite Rules

  • You are Red. Play red number tiles from 1-5. Blue CPU plays blue number tiles from 1-5.
  • The game lasts 2 rounds. Round 1 starts with Blue CPU, and round 2 starts with Red.
  • Each round begins with random center tiles already on the crosses.
  • Press Draw only to start your turn. After you place a tile, the next tile or bomb is drawn automatically.
  • Placed tiles must fit the line from the center: matching numbers, or a clean sequence with no skipped numbers.
  • Bombs remove 1 or 2 loose tiles of the bomb color, then end the turn.
  • Short lines score 25. Long lines score 50. A full small cross scores 100. A full big cross scores 200.
  • When a full cross scores, it resets with a fresh center tile. Full crosses do not also score line points.
