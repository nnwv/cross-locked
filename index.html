Cross Locked Lite

  • Score the most points by completing lines and Xs with your Red tiles while blocking the Blue CPU.
  • Each X starts with a computer-chosen center tile. Place numbers that fit that center as either the same number or a clean sequence.
  • Draw to start your turn, place any rack tile that fits, then a fresh number, wild, or bomb is drawn automatically. You may end your turn any time.
  • Wilds can stand in for any number. Bombs remove 1 or 2 loose tiles of their color from the board and end the turn.
  • Short lines score 25, long lines score 50, small Xs score 100, and the big X scores 200. A completed X does not also score its lines.
  • The game lasts 2 rounds. A round ends when the board is full, and the high score wins.
