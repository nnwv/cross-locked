Cross Locked Lite Rules

  • You are Red. Blue CPU is Blue. The game lasts 2 rounds, and each round starts with center tiles already placed.
  • Press Draw to start your turn, then place a tile next to an existing tile. After each placement, a new tile, wild tile, or bomb is drawn automatically.
  • Tiles must build from the center as matching numbers or a clean sequence. Wilds can go next to any tile and become the number they are standing in for.
  • Score completed lines and Xs. Bombs remove 1 or 2 loose tiles of their color and end the turn.
