Rules in this MVP

  • Complete lines and Xs using only your color plus jokers.
  • Small Xs use two 3-tile lines. The center Super X uses two 5-tile lines.
  • To start an empty X, your turn must place its center plus 1 or 2 matching tiles in that same X.
  • Jokers cannot go in any center. A, K, 2, and Joker cannot go in the Super X center.
  • Lock protects one completed line in an X. Unlock opens a locked line.
  • Replace actions trade an opponent tile for your matching tile. Super Replace can take a joker.
