How to Play

  1. Fill the Xs. You are Red and Blue CPU is your opponent. Complete lines and Xs using only tiles of your color.
  2. Start your turn. Press DRAW once to add a red tile to your rack. Blue CPU starts Round 1; Red starts Round 2.
  3. Choose and place. Tap a playable rack tile, then tap a red-outlined board space. Each line must use matching numbers, like 4-4-4, or a clean sequence that changes by one, like 4-5-6. Tiles normally build beside an existing tile; the far ends of the Big X may be played early when they fit the full sequence.
  4. Use WILD tiles. A WILD can fill any red-outlined legal space. Once placed, it becomes the number needed there.
  5. Keep going or stop. After every placement, the bag automatically draws your next tile. Play again or press END TURN at any time. A random bomb always matches the player who drew it, removes one or two non-center tiles of that color, and ends the turn.
  6. Score and win. A short line scores 250 points and a long line scores 500. A completed same-color Small X scores 1,000; a Big X scores 2,000. A completed X scores its X bonus instead of its line points. After two rounds, the highest combined score wins.
